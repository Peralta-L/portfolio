About Me

I am a Computer Science student currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree at the University of Maryland Global Campus, with a strong focus on growing as both a front-end and back-end developer. I have a solid foundation in programming and web technologies, including Java, C, Python, SQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I enjoy applying these skills to build practical and efficient software solutions.

Through my academic studies and hands-on learning, such as Harvard’s CS50 program, I have developed strong problem-solving abilities, analytical thinking, and a structured approach to software development. I have worked in high-responsibility environments, collaborated with international teams, and managed sensitive information with precision and accountability.

I am highly motivated to continue learning new technologies, improving my development skills, and growing into a well-rounded full-stack developer capable of building secure, scalable, and user-focused applications.
